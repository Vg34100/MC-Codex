# Quest feedback and recipe lookup

Quest tabs use a darker gray across the complete Minecraft frame and tab art. When all applicable objectives, earned rewards and required story decisions are finished, the entire frame and tab turn the same gold as a completed advancement. Unselected tabs retain their matching shaded palette. Unchosen branches and invisible recipe unlocks do not count.

A tab icon gently pulses for rewards ready to claim, a required quest awaiting first acceptance, or an available story decision. Its tooltip uses a bold heading, muted progress and color-coded reasons with counts. A completed repeatable cycle does not pulse merely because another optional cycle can be started. Accepting a new cycle makes it unfinished again until its objectives and rewards are finished. Hovering gives the icon a small eased lift; completion adds a brief icon settle and soft sheen inside the header. Animation settings disable these effects.

Creators can enable the style on a tree's root through **Quest Story & Rules → Tab → Codex quest tab**. New tabs created in the editor enable it by default. Ordinary mod and vanilla tabs retain their gray appearance unless explicitly enabled, and still turn gold on full completion. Progress and action summaries come from the server, including quests not yet revealed by Minecraft's visibility rules.

**Claim** and **Claim All** show compact reward receipts. Receiving several items at once does not displace a claim receipt with ordinary obtain notices. Notification visibility and sounds follow the existing UI settings.

When JEI is installed, hover an item inside an advancement's requirements or rewards, then use JEI's configured recipe or uses key (normally **R** or **U**). Foreground hover cards retain priority over background advancements. Existing submission and reward clicks remain available. JEI is optional; the dedicated server does not require it. A loot-only item may have no crafting recipe to show. Tag objectives expose the representative item shown in the card, rather than a tag-wide ingredient search.
